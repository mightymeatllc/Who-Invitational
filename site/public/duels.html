The Duel Card

Seven duels

Every man has one opponent on the other team. Low net over 18 wins. Strokes are the difference in playing handicaps, taken on the lowest-index holes. The two scramble pairs duel each other as a single unit.

Duel results do not affect the team result. Nothing on this card changes who wins the $800.

How a Duel Works

Sec. 02

Both men play their own ball and post their own card, so the duel costs nothing extra to track. The higher handicap receives the full difference, one shot on each of the hardest holes.

Low net over 18 wins. A tie is a tie and stays a tie unless the two of you agree to a playoff hole at the pool, which is between you.

Side money is private. Wager $20, dinner, or the whole pot — the committee holds none of it, records none of it, and settles none of it. Bring the man his money yourself.

Four of these seven are dead even at scratch and six are inside five strokes, so the committee has removed the last available excuse. There is no bad matchup on this card. There is only bad golf, and it will have a name attached to it by six o'clock.
