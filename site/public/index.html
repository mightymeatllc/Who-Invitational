The card

The Game

Sec. 01

Two teams of eight. One round of individual net stroke play. Six men a side play their own ball; two play as a 2-man scramble pair, which gives each team seven scoring units — six individual net scores and one scramble net score.

Team score is the best SIX of those seven. The worst unit on each team is dropped, counts for nothing, and is named on the scoreboard by name. That is not an accident of the format. That is the format.

The scramble pair can be the dropped unit. Two men, one ball, still worthless — a live possibility on both sides.

Max score on any hole is triple bogey. Playing handicaps are 90% of index, capped at a 25 index. Everyone plays Blue except the two scramble pairs, who play White and give back three strokes for the privilege.

String has already asked, in writing, how to reach the triple-bogey cap deliberately and how to land on the drop score on purpose. Both questions have been noted in the file. He is playing in the scramble, so for eighteen holes he only has to convince Terb, who cannot see where the ball went anyway.

The Money

Sec. 02

Four of the sixteen are carrying a committee estimate instead of an established index. One of them has a golf simulator in his garage and says he never plays. Another moved his index three strokes last month and offered no explanation. They are duelling each other, which is the closest thing to justice this committee could arrange.

Carts & Tee Groups

Sec. 03

Groups go off ten minutes apart. Carts do not follow the duels and never will — if you want to argue about that, take it up with the committee, which settles nothing. The pairings were made carefully, by which the committee means they were made with full knowledge of what would happen and no intention of preventing it.

Afterward

Sec. 04

Pool party at Toe's Abode when the cards are in. The pool has no membership committee, no waitlist, no application process and no standards of any kind — it is open to all sixteen men, which for at least one of them is the entire problem.

The host has never cooked anything in his life, so dinner will arrive by app, in bags, carried to the door by a stranger. Bring your own towel. He will have paid someone for those too.

Two things are guaranteed before midnight. Toe and Adam will get into it, because they have never once failed to, and now there is a swimming pool for the second act. And Ron will finally be in a pool that did not ask him for a membership, standing five feet from the man who has spent three years telling him about the one that did.
